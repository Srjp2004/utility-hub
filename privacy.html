Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

UtilityHub is designed as a browser-first utility platform. For the core tools, calculations and processing are performed in your browser and do not require an account or a server upload of your tool input.

Information you provide

If you contact UtilityHub by email, we receive the information you choose to include in that message, such as your email address, message and attachments. We use it to respond to your request, investigate reported problems and provide support.

Browser-based tool inputs

Core calculator, text and image utilities are designed to process their working inputs locally in your browser. You should still avoid entering confidential information into any online service unless you are comfortable with its handling.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current core platform does not require an account. If analytics, advertising, affiliate tracking, cookies or other third-party services are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant providers, purposes, data practices and available choices before those services are relied upon.

Third-party links and services

UtilityHub may link to external websites or services. Their privacy practices are controlled by those providers, not UtilityHub. Review the applicable provider policy before sharing information with an external service.

Data requests

For privacy questions or requests relating to information you sent directly to UtilityHub, contact utilityhub.support@gmail.com.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the platform, processing practices or third-party services materially change. The latest version will be published on this page.
