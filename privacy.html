Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

UtilityHub is designed as a browser-first utility platform. This policy explains the current approach to information handled through the website. If the platform adds a service that materially changes data handling, this policy should be updated before that service is introduced.

Information processed by the tools

Core calculators and many browser-based utilities process the values you enter in your browser. They do not require an account for normal use. Where a tool works entirely locally, the entered data is not intentionally sent to a UtilityHub server by the tool itself.

Contact messages

If you email UtilityHub, the information you include in your message, including your email address and the contents of your request, is received by the email service used to operate that mailbox. Use the contact addresses only for support, corrections, feedback and legitimate enquiries.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

UtilityHub may use analytics, advertising or affiliate services as the platform develops. Such services can involve cookies, local storage, device information, approximate location or other technical data depending on the provider. When these services are enabled, this policy should be updated to describe the specific providers, purposes and available choices before relying on this page as a complete description of those practices.

At present, this page does not claim that a particular advertising or analytics provider is active. Third-party provider policies may also apply when their services are used.

Third-party links and services

Links to third-party websites are provided for navigation or utility. UtilityHub does not control the privacy practices of external sites. Review their policies before submitting information.

Security and retention

UtilityHub aims to minimize unnecessary data collection. Contact correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain support records or meet applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers or other sensitive information in a support email unless specifically required through a trusted process.

Your questions

For privacy questions or requests about information you have sent to UtilityHub, email utilityhub.support@gmail.com.
