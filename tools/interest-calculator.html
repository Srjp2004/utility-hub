How the Interest Calculator works

Enter the principal, rate and duration and compare the calculated interest with the balance you expect from your chosen interest model.

Important details and limitations

Simple interest applies the rate to the original principal. Compound interest allows accumulated interest to participate in later calculations, depending on the compounding schedule.

Practical guidance

Actual savings, loans and investments can use different conventions, fees, taxes and compounding frequencies. Treat the result as an estimate.
