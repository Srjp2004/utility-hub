Age Calculator

Calculate age in years, months and days from a date of birth.

Using the Age Calculator

Use the Age Calculator to calculate age from a date of birth using calendar dates.

How the Age Calculator works

Select the birth date and let the calculator compare it with the current date available to the browser. Calendar boundaries can affect the years, months and days shown.

Important details and limitations

Age calculations are date-sensitive. The result is intended for general information and should not be treated as an official identity, eligibility or legal record.

Practical guidance

For formal applications, use the date convention and documentation required by the relevant organization rather than relying only on an online calculator.
