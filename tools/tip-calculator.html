Tip & Bill Split Calculator

Calculate a tip and split the total bill between people.

How does tip splitting work?

The tip is calculated from the bill and selected tip percentage. The bill plus tip is then divided by the number of people.

The result is a mathematical estimate. Local tipping customs and service charges can vary.
