Tip & Bill Split Calculator

Calculate a tip and split the total bill between people.

Using the Tip & Bill Split Calculator

Use the Tip & Bill Split Calculator to calculate a tip and divide the resulting bill across a selected number of people.

How the Tip & Bill Split Calculator works

Enter the bill, choose the tip percentage and specify the number of people. Review the total before splitting it.

Important details and limitations

The result is a mathematical split and does not know local tipping customs, mandatory service charges, taxes or restaurant-specific policies.

Practical guidance

If a bill already includes a service charge or tax, check the receipt before adding another percentage so the calculation matches the amount you actually intend to pay.
