Count weekdays between two dates without requiring an external service.

Using the Business Days Calculator

Use the Business Days Calculator to count weekdays between two dates.

How the Business Days Calculator works

Select the start and end dates. The calculator counts Monday through Friday using its documented inclusive date behavior.

Important details and limitations

Public holidays are not automatically excluded because holiday calendars vary by country, region, organization and year. If holidays matter, subtract the applicable dates using an authoritative calendar.

Practical guidance

For contracts, payroll or government deadlines, verify the required business-day convention with the relevant authority or agreement.
