Case Converter

Convert text to uppercase, lowercase, title case or sentence case in your browser.

Using the Case Converter

Use the Case Converter to change text between uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case.

How the Case Converter works

Paste or type text, choose the desired transformation and review the result before copying it into your document or application.

Important details and limitations

Case conversion is a formatting operation. It does not understand every brand name, acronym, language-specific capitalization rule or editorial style.

Practical guidance

The conversion runs locally in the browser, making it useful for quick cleanup of ordinary text without creating an account.
