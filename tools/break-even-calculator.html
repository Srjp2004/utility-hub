Using the Break-Even Calculator

Use the Break-Even Calculator to estimate the sales volume or revenue required to cover fixed and variable costs.

How the Break-Even Calculator works

Enter fixed costs, selling price and variable cost per unit. The result identifies the point at which modeled revenue covers modeled costs.

Important details and limitations

The calculation assumes the inputs remain stable. If prices, variable costs, product mix or fixed costs change, the practical break-even point changes too.

Practical guidance

Use the result as a planning scenario. For a real business, reconcile the assumptions with current pricing, capacity, taxes and operating costs.
