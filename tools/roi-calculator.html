Using the ROI Calculator

Use the ROI Calculator to estimate return on investment and net gain from an initial investment.

How the ROI Calculator works

Enter the investment amount and resulting return values. Review the net gain and percentage together rather than using the percentage without its underlying amounts.

Important details and limitations

ROI is a simple comparison and does not automatically account for time, financing costs, taxes, inflation or risk. Two investments with the same ROI can have very different circumstances.

Practical guidance

For investment or business decisions, use verified records and consider the time period and costs that the simple formula leaves out.
