Using the Percentage Calculator

Use the Percentage Calculator for percentage-of-value questions, discounts, grades, business metrics and everyday calculations.

How the Percentage Calculator works

Enter the values requested by the calculator and check the result against the units and context of your original numbers.

Important details and limitations

A percentage is a proportion expressed per 100. For example, 25 out of 200 is 12.5%. The calculator is intended for straightforward numerical work and does not perform currency conversion.

Practical guidance

Check the original values before relying on a result. Rounding can matter when percentages are used in invoices, reports or financial calculations.
