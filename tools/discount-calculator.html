Discount Calculator

Calculate sale price, discount amount and final price online.

Using the Discount Calculator

Use the Discount Calculator to find a discount amount and the resulting sale price from an original price and discount percentage.

How the Discount Calculator works

Enter the original price and discount rate. The calculation shows how much is removed from the original amount and what remains after the discount.

Important details and limitations

This calculator does not convert currencies. Enter the price in the currency you are already using and apply any separate tax, shipping or service-charge rules yourself.

Practical guidance

For stacked promotions, check each discount rule carefully because two percentage discounts are not normally the same as adding their percentages together.
