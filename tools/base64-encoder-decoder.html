Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Encode or decode text as Base64 directly in your browser.

Using the Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Use the Base64 Encoder & Decoder to encode or decode text locally in your browser.

How the Base64 Encoder & Decoder works

Enter text and choose encode or decode. The encoder handles Unicode text through UTF-8 bytes, while invalid Base64 input is rejected during decoding.

Important details and limitations

Base64 is an encoding format, not encryption. Anyone who has the encoded data can generally decode it, so do not use Base64 as a way to protect secrets.

Practical guidance

For sensitive information, use an appropriate authenticated encryption design instead of relying on an encoding tool.
