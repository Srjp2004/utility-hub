HIGH-INTENT DECISION TOOL

QuotePulse

Before you pay or approve an estimate, paste it here. QuotePulse looks for document-level problems such as mismatched totals, unusually large deposits, vague scope language and missing payment terms.

What QuotePulse checks

It works locally in your browser and focuses on information that is visible inside the quote. It can detect line-item amounts, an explicit total, deposit language, duplicate-looking charges, vague wording and missing terms such as warranty, cancellation and change-order rules.

What it does not claim

QuotePulse does not know the correct market price for a job. A high or low price can be reasonable depending on location, materials, urgency, quality and scope. Use the result to ask better questions, not as proof that a provider is overcharging.

Good questions to ask before approval

Ask whether the total is all-in, what happens when scope changes, what the deposit covers, how refunds work, and what warranty or guarantee is included. Keeping these answers in writing can make later disputes easier to understand.

Privacy

The current tool does not upload the pasted quote to a server. Your text stays in the browser while you use the page.
