GOOD TO KNOW

Before you use a result

Are the tools free?

Yes. The current UtilityHub tools are free and do not require sign-up.

Do you upload my data?

Core browser tools are designed to process ordinary inputs locally where practical. Check each tool page for its specific limits and processing notes.

Are results guaranteed to be exact?

Results depend on the values and assumptions entered. For financial, health, legal or other consequential decisions, verify important results with an appropriate professional or official source.
