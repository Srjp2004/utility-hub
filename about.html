ABOUT UTILITYHUB

Practical tools, built for everyday use.

UtilityHub is a global-first collection of free browser-based calculators and utilities for everyday life, work, study and digital tasks.

What we focus on

Useful first

We improve existing tools based on real use cases, edge cases and measurable defects rather than adding tools simply to make the catalog larger.

Clear results

Inputs, assumptions, validation and results should be understandable. Where a calculation is an estimate, UtilityHub aims to say so.

Privacy-aware

Core browser-based tools are designed to process inputs locally where practical, without requiring an account.

Quality and limitations

UtilityHub uses automated tests, browser testing and security checks as part of ongoing engineering. A passing calculation does not make a result professional advice or a guarantee of a real-world outcome.

For financial, medical, legal, tax, employment or other consequential decisions, verify important results with the appropriate official source or qualified professional.

Contact

Found a calculation issue, accessibility problem or useful improvement? Contact the UtilityHub team.
